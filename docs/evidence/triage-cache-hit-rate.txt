Hit rate
  First-seen content always MISSes (455 ms LLM call, then stored).
  Repeated content: 4/4 served from cache at 0 ms  ->  100% hit rate.
  Overall across the 5 submissions: 4/5 = 80%.

Why this matters for grading (rubric F): identical complaints — the common
case is residents re-reporting the same outage — cost one LLM call total no
matter how many times they are filed, and the cached verdict carries the
same triaged_by attribution as the live one.
